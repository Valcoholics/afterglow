# The Three-Layer Model: How Afterglow Works

---

## Layer 1: Timeline View (Choose Your Context)

**What it shows:** Five time-of-day phases

```
Warm-up  |  Pool Party  |  Festival Brunch  |  Post-game  |  Sunrise Reset
```

**Why these five?**
These represent the real DJ scenarios people face:
- **Warm-up:** Early, setting the vibe (6–9 pm)
- **Pool Party:** Peak fun, uplifting (4–7 pm)
- **Festival Brunch:** Daytime, social (10 am–2 pm)
- **Post-game:** Late night, energy variable (11 pm–2 am)
- **Sunrise Reset:** Dawn, contemplative (5–8 am)

**User action:** Click "Pool Party" → constellation loads

---

## Layer 2: Constellation View (See Your Library in Context)

**What it shows:** All tracks for the selected phase in a **spatial arrangement**.

The space is organized by **three DJ decision-making dimensions:**

### X-Axis: Journey Role
**What does this track DO in a set?**

```
Opener → Bridge → Reset → Home Stretch
(0)      (1)      (2)       (3)
```

- **Opener:** Sets initial tone. Accessible, welcoming.
- **Bridge:** Transitions smoothly. Shifts energy or mood.
- **Reset:** Recalibrates energy. Gives breathing room.
- **Home Stretch:** Climax or close. Peak moment or wind-down.

### Y-Axis: Crowd State
**Who's here? What's their attention?**

```
Arrivals
   ↓
Lock-in
   ↓
Wanderers
```

- **Arrivals:** People are still coming. Lower energy, high attention to new arrivals.
- **Lock-in:** Everyone's here. Focused, high engagement. Peak moment.
- **Wanderers:** Some people have left or moved. Smaller groups, conversation. Flexibility needed.

### Color/Grouping: Time/Setting
**When is this track relevant?**

Pool Party tracks = warm tones (oranges, golds)  
Sunrise Reset = cool tones (blues)  
Festival Brunch = vibrant tones

## The Spatial Grid (Pool Party Example)

```
                Opener      Bridge      Reset      Home Stretch
Arrivals (0)   Track A     Track B     Track C      Track D
Lock-in (1)    Track E     Track F     Track G      Track H
Wanderers (2)  Track I     Track J     Track K      Track L
```

Each cell contains 3–8 tracks that match that (Role, Crowd State) combination.

**Every position is justified.** You can point to any track and say: "This is here because it's a Bridge track that works for Lock-in crowd." Traceable, not mysterious.

---

## Why Spatial?

**List browsing:** Scroll, read titles, make decisions  
**Spatial browsing:** Scan layout, see relationships, make connections

Example:
- Hovering over a Bridge track → nearby tracks brighten (shows what comes before/after)
- Visual proximity = musical adjacency
- You discover tracks you'd forgotten

**Why this matters:**
- You see the **reason** the track is positioned where it is
- You understand the **logic** (not a mystery)
- You can validate: "Yes, that makes sense" or "Actually, this should be Reset instead"

## Key Design Principles

### 1. Intentional, Not Algorithmic
Positions are **hardcoded or manually arranged**, not generated by an algorithm. Why? Because DJ curation is honest; algorithm is arbitrary. You can defend every position.

### 2. Spatial is Functional
The arrangement isn't decorative. Proximity = musical relationship. Hover interaction proves it works.

### 3. DJ Agency is Central
System organizes **your** library **your** way. You decide all tags. No recommendations. No automation. You see your taste reflected back to you.

### 4. Three Dimensions Suffice
Time/Setting × Journey Role × Crowd State = complete DJ decision-making model. Not reductive. Not over-engineered.

### 5. Rediscovery is the Hero
The magic moment is finding a track with play count < 5 that's genuinely great. The system creates space for that moment.

## What's NOT Here

- **Audio playback:** This is a prep tool, not a player
- **Algorithmic recommendations:** DJ decides; system organizes
- **AI tagging:** Manual curation is honest and authentic
- **Other phases** (yet): Pool Party is the demo; proves concept works for one context
- **Search:** Spatial discovery is the primary experience

---

## What Happens Next

Once judges see this three-layer model working for Pool Party:
- **Obvious roadmap:** Same approach for Warm-up, Festival Brunch, Post-game, Sunrise Reset
- **Natural expansion:** User customization (own categories, own contexts)
- **Integration ready:** Design prepared for djay SDK or other tools (future)

**But MVP is just Pool Party.** One beautiful phase > three half-finished phases.

---

## Success Definition

A DJ looking at the Pool Party constellation should immediately think:

**"Oh, this is how I actually organize when I prep. This is faster than scrolling. And I found something I'd forgotten."**

If they think that, the concept works.
