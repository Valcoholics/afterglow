# Afterglow: Project Overview

**What is Afterglow?**  
A spatial DJ library browsing system that organizes music by **how DJs actually prepare for gigs**, not by metadata alone.

**One sentence:**  
Organize your library by timeline of day + performance contexts (who's here? what role?) instead of genre/BPM folders.

---

## The Problem (Real and Specific)

DJs often get spontaneous gigs: "Can you play a pool party in 2 hours?" or "We need someone for festival brunch." Current library organization (genre folders, BPM sorting) doesn't match the actual decision-making process: "When would I play this? Why? What's the crowd state?"

This mismatch is painful for emerging DJs who feel blocked by their own library.

---

## The Solution (Three Layers)

1. **Timeline View** — Choose your scenario (Pool Party, Sunrise Reset, Post-game, etc.)
2. **Constellation View** — See all your tracks for that context in a spatial arrangement
   - X-axis: Journey Role (Opener → Home Stretch)
   - Y-axis: Crowd State (Arrivals → Wanderers)
   - Color/grouping: Time/Setting
3. **Track Details** — Click to see why the system placed it there (artist, tags, play count)

---

## The Value

- **For DJs:** Prep a spontaneous gig in 10 minutes instead of 30+. Rediscover forgotten material. Feel confident.
- **For the concept:** Organize libraries by DJ thinking, not traditional metadata categories.

---

## MVP Scope (Hackathon)

- **One phase:** Pool Party constellation (fully developed)
- **Real library:** 150 tracks, manually tagged, Creative Commons/royalty-free
- **Three views:** Timeline → Constellation → Track details
- **No audio:** Prep tool, not playback tool
- **No AI:** DJ decides; system organizes
- **Timeline:** 24-hour sprint (library curation + UI build + polish)

---

## Why This Matters

An emerging DJ who feels blocked by library organization stops DJing. A confident DJ discovers cross-genre combinations they wouldn't have found otherwise. Organization directly impacts creativity and performance quality.

---

## Key Decisions (Greenlit, No Debate)

✓ Pool Party only (not all five phases)  
✓ Spatial positions are intentional, not algorithmic  
✓ Real tracks (authentic demo library)  
✓ Three dimensions, nine tags (decided)  
✓ Manual tagging (you listen, you decide)  
✓ "DJ decision-making dimensions" (mandatory language, not "mood")  
✓ Rediscovery as core UX mechanic  
✓ 24-hour sprint with clear role split
